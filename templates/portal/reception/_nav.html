{% comment %} The reception nav bar — one component, shared by every reception screen, so they read as one application rather than a page each one bolted on for itself. `nav_active` names the screen we are on, so its own entry is left out of the bar rather than offered as somewhere to go: a page never needs a link back to itself, and a bar that showed every destination every time would mean reading past your own current page on every screen to find the one you actually want. `show_doctor_filter` puts the All-doctors select at the head of the bar.
