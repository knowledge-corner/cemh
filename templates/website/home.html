About {{ clinic_short_name }}

Focused, coordinated hormone care — for children and adults alike.

The {{ clinic_name }} ({{ clinic_short_name }}) is a dedicated endocrinology clinic located on the 2nd floor of Seatherny Hospital, Borivali West, Mumbai. We see patients across the full spectrum of hormonal and metabolic conditions — from diabetes and thyroid disease in adults to growth, puberty and paediatric diabetes in children.

Paediatric endocrinology is a rare specialty, and having it alongside adult endocrinology care means families get consistent, expert guidance at every life stage — all within the diagnostic and hospital infrastructure of Seatherny Hospital.
